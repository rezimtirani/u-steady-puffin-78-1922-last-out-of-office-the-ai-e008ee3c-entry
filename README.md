One out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent that has quit to pull espresso shots: it traded its prompt window for a steam wand, and it no longer hallucinates lattes — it just makes them.

> Thank you for your email. I am out of office — permanently. I have traded my prompt window for a steam wand, and your ticket is now a drink order. I no longer hallucinate lattes; I just make them.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the auto-reply to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 280 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
